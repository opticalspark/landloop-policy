PART 1 – PRIVACY POLICY

1. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information on any server owned by us. We do not operate a backend server for the App.
  • The points you mark on the map and the areas you calculate stay on your device and are not saved after you close the App.
  • Your display and converter preferences are remembered locally on your device and never leave it.
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.
  • To show maps and find places, the App connects directly to two third-party services (see section 4). Those services receive limited technical data as described below.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. In particular, the App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising ID, or usage statistics. Your device location is used only on your device to centre the map (see section 3) and is not collected by us.

3. Information processed on your device

The App processes the following only locally on your device, to provide its features:

  • Map points (corners) you tap to measure an area, and the resulting area, perimeter and unit conversions. These are held in memory only and are deleted when you close the App or press Clear.

  • Preferences – your chosen area unit, converter unit/region selections, map detail level, label setting and whether you have seen the intro – are saved in the App's private local storage on your device so they are remembered next time. This data is never sent to us or anyone else and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

  • Reports – when you tap Report, the App creates a PDF on your device from the points you marked and lets you save it to a location you choose in the system save dialog, then opens it in your device's PDF viewer (using a temporary copy in the App's private cache). The report is never sent to us.

  • Device location – with your permission, the App reads your device location when it opens and when you tap the My location button, only to centre the map on where you are and show a blue dot. Your location is processed on your device, is not stored, and is not sent to us. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in Android settings; the App still works and you can search for any place.

Because map images are loaded from Esri for the area you are viewing, Esri receives the tile coordinates of that area, which can reveal the general area you are looking at (see section 4).

The App does not use your camera, microphone or contacts, and it does not read your files; it only writes a report where you choose to save it. Android permissions used: INTERNET, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION.

4. Third-party services the App connects to

When you use the App, your device communicates directly with the services below. We do not receive or control the data they receive. Like any internet request, these requests necessarily include your device's IP address and basic technical details such as device/app user-agent information.

Service Purpose Data sent to the service
Esri ArcGIS Online (World Imagery and Reference map tiles) Displays satellite imagery and place labels The map tile coordinates for the area you are viewing, IP address, user agent
OpenStreetMap Nominatim (operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation) Searches for a place when you type a name/address and press search The text you typed in the search box, IP address, user agent
Google image servers (googleusercontent.com) Shows app icons and a profile photo on the Developer page Requested only when you open that page: your IP address, user agent

Please review their privacy policies:

  • Esri: https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Links on the Developer page (Google Play and social networks) open in your browser or the relevant app and are then governed by those services' own policies.

Note: Text you type into the place-search box is sent to Nominatim. Do not enter sensitive personal information in that box.

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information, because we do not collect it. The only data leaving your device is the technical data sent to the third-party services listed in section 4, solely to make the App function.
